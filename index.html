Survival & hygiene

Fire safety

Use an established grill or permitted fire pit when possible. Keep fires small and attended. Never use gasoline or light a fire inside a tent, vehicle, building, under a bridge, or other enclosed/covered place. Extinguish completely.

No shower?

• Bottle wash: clean water + washcloth + small amount of soap. Focus on face/neck, armpits, groin and feet.
• In a permitted public restroom, use a damp cloth and leave the sink/area clean.
• Wipes help between showers. Dry feet carefully and change wet socks.
• Keep a small hygiene kit: soap, cloth, toothbrush, toothpaste, deodorant, wipes, spare socks and small towel.

Water

Ottawa has drinking fountains/refill points. Only use fountains intended for drinking. Do not treat decorative fountains or river water as safe drinking water.

Stay warmer & drier

• Keep clothing and bedding dry whenever possible.
• Insulate yourself from cold ground.
• Change wet socks/clothes as soon as possible.
• Save phone battery: lower brightness, close unused apps and keep key information stored offline.
